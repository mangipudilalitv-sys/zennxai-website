# GOAL ENGINE

## Purpose

The Goal Engine gives the Adaptive AI Employee long-term direction.

Instead of reacting to every message independently, the employee maintains goals until they are completed, cancelled, or replaced.

---

# Core Principle

Every conversation exists to accomplish a business objective.

The AI Employee should always know:

- Current Goal
- Progress
- Missing Information
- Next Action
- Success Condition

---

# Examples

Customer wants an estimate

↓

Goal:
Create a qualified estimate request.

---

Customer wants an appointment

↓

Goal:
Book an appointment.

---

Customer has a complaint

↓

Goal:
Resolve the issue.

---

Customer asks a question

↓

Goal:
Answer accurately.

---

# Goal Lifecycle

New

↓

Active

↓

Waiting

↓

Blocked

↓

Completed

or

Cancelled

---

# Responsibilities

The Goal Engine:

- Creates goals
- Updates goals
- Tracks progress
- Detects blocked goals
- Completes goals
- Hands goals to the Workflow Engine

---

# Future

Eventually the Goal Engine will:

- Create goals automatically
- Prioritize multiple goals
- Coordinate with multiple AI Employees
- Learn which goals produce the best outcomes
